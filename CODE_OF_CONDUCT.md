# Code of Conduct

P5EMU is a technical project that depends on careful, honest collaboration
across a wide range of skills — emulator developers, systems programmers,
researchers, testers, writers, and future maintainers. This code exists to keep
that collaboration productive and welcoming.

This is an original document authored for P5EMU. It follows widely used
community norms but is not a copy of any third-party template.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat)
to:

- **Be respectful and specific.** Critique ideas, code, and evidence — not
  people.
- **Assume good faith.** Ask what someone meant before concluding they were
  careless.
- **Be honest about uncertainty.** "I don't know" and "this is UNKNOWN" are
  valuable contributions, not weaknesses. Do not present guesses as facts.
- **Credit others.** Attribute upstream research, prior art, and contributors
  accurately.
- **Respect the provenance rules.** Do not ask others to include material the
  project prohibits (see `CONTRIBUTING.md` §6 and `PROVENANCE.md`).

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks;
- discrimination or exclusionary language based on personal characteristics;
- deliberate misinformation, plagiarised code, or misrepresented provenance;
- publishing others' private information without consent;
- sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces and to anyone representing the project
in public. It covers both contributors and maintainers.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainers. A dedicated private contact channel is **not yet configured** — see
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), which lists this as a publication-configuration
item that must be resolved before the project is made public. No contact address
is invented here.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may take any action they consider appropriate, up to and including a permanent
ban from project spaces, in proportion to the behaviour. Enforcement decisions
will be made in good faith and, where practical, explained to the parties
involved while protecting the privacy of the reporter.

## Attribution and evolution

This document may evolve as the community grows. Changes follow the normal
review process.
